Projects

Superbug

2026 · Unity, C# · Solo · MSc dissertation

A top-down action game where you play a bacterium in a wastewater treatment plant, stealing DNA from other microbes to gain their abilities. I built it for my master's dissertation on using humour to communicate microbiology. The game is the instrument of a study where players answer questionnaires before and after playing.

  • Energy (ATP) system that every action draws from: moving, abilities and healing.
  • Pilus grapple that pulls plasmids off other microbes. Plasmids have to be assimilated from a menu before they decay, and each species drops the DNA for its own ability.
  • Keyboard and mouse, Xbox and PlayStation controller support.

Download on itch.io (Windows)

Scoundrel Tower

2026 · Unity, C# · Solo · WebGL

A roguelike version of Zach Gage and Kurt Bieg's solitaire card game Scoundrel. Each room is dealt from your deck, and you choose the order in which to fight monsters, equip weapons and drink potions. There's no score: a run is measured by how deep you get.

  • Rules for jokers, vouchers, consumables, weapon forging and bosses that change the rules of a floor.
  • Seeded daily run that is the same for everyone, autosave, English and Portuguese.
  • WebGL build that plays in desktop and mobile browsers.

Play in the browser

MTG Life Counter

2026 · JavaScript, HTML, CSS · Solo · Web app

A life counter for Magic: The Gathering, built for a phone left in the middle of the table: the players at the top see their counters rotated to face them. It has no framework or build step: about 5,000 lines of plain JavaScript, split into state, UI and API modules.

  • Five modes for 2 to 8 players: Commander, 1v1, free, teams, and a 6-player Battle Royale with zones, events and loot.
  • Commander search and card art through the Scryfall REST API.
  • Installable PWA. A service worker caches the app so it works offline, and games and player profiles are saved in localStorage.

Source on GitHub

Unbreakable Phone

2025 · Unreal Engine 5 · Team of 5 · MSc project

A first-person game where Dona Irene, an elderly woman, takes down a greedy tech company with her old phone and its special powers.

My part: I programmed the throwing mechanics and the phone's special abilities, and designed and built all the menus and UI. I also worked on the story, sound design and concept art.

Lords of Conflict

2024 · Unity, C# · Solo · Prototype

A turn-based tactics game inspired by Fire Emblem and Advance Wars, which I started on my own before the master's. It's a prototype: the systems work, but it isn't a finished game. I wrote all the code; the art and music are third-party assets.

  • Grid movement and combat between units with their own stats.
  • A shop where players spend money to recruit new units.
  • Match setup with map size and number of players, plus a Battle Royale mode.

Play in the browser

Newsdle

2025 · HTML5 · Team of 2 · MSc course project

A browser game about spotting misinformation. You moderate a news site over four days and decide which articles to publish and which to reject. Each day focuses on something different: checking sources, clickbait and manipulation, and misleading statistics and graphs.

  • Sources can be marked as trusted or blacklisted, and that list carries over between articles.
  • Scoring is a balance between audience and credibility, with bonuses for fast, correct decisions.

Play in the browser
