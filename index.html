Superbug

  • 2026
  • Unity, C#
  • Solo
  • MSc dissertation

A top-down action game where you play a bacterium in a wastewater treatment plant, stealing DNA from other microbes to gain their abilities. I built it for my master's dissertation on using humour to communicate microbiology. The game is the instrument of a study where players answer questionnaires before and after playing.

  • Energy (ATP) system that every action draws from: moving, abilities and healing.
  • Pilus grapple that pulls plasmids off other microbes. Plasmids have to be assimilated from a menu before they decay, and each species drops the DNA for its own ability.
  • Keyboard and mouse, Xbox and PlayStation controller support, in a browser build and a Windows download.

Scoundrel Tower

  • 2026
  • Unity, C#
  • Solo
  • WebGL

A roguelike version of Zach Gage and Kurt Bieg's solitaire card game Scoundrel. Each room is dealt from your deck, and you choose the order in which to fight monsters, equip weapons and drink potions. There's no score: a run is measured by how deep you get.

  • Rules for jokers, vouchers, consumables, weapon forging and bosses that change the rules of a floor.
  • Seeded daily run that is the same for everyone, autosave, English and Portuguese.
  • WebGL build that plays in desktop and mobile browsers.

MTG Life Counter

  • 2026
  • JavaScript, HTML, CSS
  • Solo
  • Web app

A life counter for Magic: The Gathering, built for a phone or tablet left in the middle of the table: the players on the far side see their counters rotated to face them. It also keeps a playgroup's history, saving every finished game against the decks and players that took part. It has no framework or build step: about 10,000 lines of plain JavaScript, split into state, data and UI modules.

  • Five modes for 2 to 8 players: Commander, 1v1, free, teams, and a 6-player Battle Royale with zones, events and loot. It tracks commander damage, partner commanders, poison and other counters, and times each turn.
  • Deck and player profiles with win rates, head-to-head records and an ELO ranking with leagues. The charts are drawn without a chart library.
  • Commander search and card art through the Scryfall REST API.
  • Optional cloud sync for a playgroup, stored in Supabase and joined with a group code. Two phones can also merge their data by QR code.
  • Installable PWA that works offline, in English and Portuguese, with 33 Playwright tests.

Tempo

  • 2026
  • TypeScript, React
  • Solo
  • Web app

A weather app for Portugal, built on the open data of IPMA, the Portuguese weather service. It shows the current conditions, an hourly chart, a 10-day forecast and the official warnings for the selected place, and is designed to feel like a native iPhone app when installed on the home screen.

  • Hourly chart for temperature, rain and wind, drawn in SVG without a chart library. It follows the current time and can be scrubbed by touch or with the arrow keys.
  • Touch interactions: swipe sideways between saved places, pull to refresh, sheets that drag to dismiss and drag to reorder the list of places.
  • A Cloudflare Worker proxies IPMA and Open-Meteo, validates each response before caching it and serves the last good copy when a source is down. The forecast source can be changed in the settings.
  • Animated sky behind the current conditions, drawn in a WebGL shader, with a static fallback and support for reduced motion.
  • Installable PWA with light and dark themes, in English and Portuguese, with 28 Vitest tests run in CI.

Minho Defenders

  • 2026
  • JavaScript, Canvas 2D
  • Solo
  • AI game jam

A tower defense set in 1368 on the River Minho, the old border between Portugal and Castile. Castilian boats cross the river to besiege four real fortress towns: Valença, Monção, Cerveira and the Forte da Lagarteira. I made it for Slapjam AI, a game jam for games built with AI tools.

How it was made: The code was written with Claude, which I directed and play-tested. No image or audio models were used: the art is drawn by Canvas 2D and SVG code, and the music and sound effects are generated with the Web Audio API.

  • Four maps, four tower types with two specialisations each, two heroines who level up, and a figure from local legend for each town.
  • Campaign, free play, endless mode and a daily challenge. Co-op for two players on one screen, or online with a room code.
  • A single HTML file with no build step that plays offline, in English and Portuguese.

Newsdle

  • 2025
  • HTML5
  • Team of 2
  • MSc course project

A browser game about spotting misinformation. You moderate a news site over four days and decide which articles to publish and which to reject. Each day focuses on something different: checking sources, clickbait and manipulation, and misleading statistics and graphs.

  • Sources can be marked as trusted or blacklisted, and that list carries over between articles.
  • Scoring is a balance between audience and credibility, with bonuses for fast, correct decisions.
